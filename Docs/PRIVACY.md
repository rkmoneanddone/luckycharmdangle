# Lucky Dangle Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 14, 2026

Lucky Dangle is a Windows desktop application developed by Rohit Kumar
Mallick.

Lucky Dangle is designed to operate locally on your Windows computer.
The application does not require you to create an account.

## Information We Collect

Lucky Dangle does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share
personal information.

The application does not require access to:

- Your contacts
- Your location
- Your microphone
- Your camera
- Your files or documents
- Your online accounts

## Information Stored Locally

Lucky Dangle stores a small amount of application preference data on
your computer so that the application can remember your experience.

This may include:

- Your selected dangle
- Your Lucky Dangle window position

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted
to the developer.

## Internet Access

The current version of Lucky Dangle does not require an online account
or cloud service for its core functionality.

## Support Emails

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose
to include in your message.

Your email will be used to respond to your support request or feedback.
We do not sell or intentionally share support correspondence for
advertising purposes.

## Third-Party Services

The current version of Lucky Dangle does not intentionally transmit
application usage data to third-party analytics or advertising services.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Lucky Dangle gains new features
or when legal or regulatory requirements change.

The latest version will be made available with the application or at
the privacy-policy location provided for the Microsoft Store listing.
