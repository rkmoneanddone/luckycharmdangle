# About Lucky Dangle

## Lucky Dangle

Lucky Dangle is a lightweight Windows desktop companion that brings
colorful lucky charms and playful interaction to your desktop.

Choose a dangle, move it anywhere on your screen, pull and release the
charm, enjoy its swing animation, or let Lucky Dangle choose a random
charm for you.

### Features

- Multiple lucky dangles and collections
- Random Dangle selection
- Interactive pull and release physics
- Swing interaction
- Move the dangle anywhere on the desktop
- Remembers your selected dangle
- Remembers your preferred desktop position
- Works locally on Windows
